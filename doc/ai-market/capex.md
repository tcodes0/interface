# Indicator 1 — U.S. Hyperscaler Capex

Scope: Microsoft, Amazon, Alphabet, Meta, Oracle. Tracks quarterly capex and the direction of
spending growth. This is explicitly a **U.S. hyperscaler capex proxy**, not global AI capex — the
primary **demand-side indicator** in the dashboard (see [dashboard.md](./dashboard.md)).

Updated manually on a quarterly cadence, on each company's earnings release. Append-only, oldest
first — never edit or delete a historical row.

**Methodology note:** the quantitative figures below are each company's **cash paid for
property/equipment** (SEC XBRL tag `PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment`, or
`PaymentsToAcquireProductiveAssets` for Amazon, which uses that tag for the same cash-flow-statement
line). This is the most consistently-defined figure across companies and pulls straight from
audited 10-Q/10-K filings via SEC EDGAR's XBRL company-concept API. It **excludes finance-lease
principal payments**. Company guidance commentary ("Fwd Guidance" column, and the outlook figures
quoted on earnings calls) often uses a broader "capital expenditures" figure that **includes**
finance-lease principal payments — e.g. Microsoft's Q4 FY26 call cited $41B in "capital
expenditures" for the quarter, vs. $35.8B cash paid for PP&E per the 10-K. See
[updating.md](./updating.md) for more on this and other lessons learned gathering this sheet.

(`data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyconcept/CIK.../us-gaap/PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment.json`,

## Notes

- Forward guidance isn't consistently given by every company every quarter — record when
  available, don't manufacture an estimate.
- No scoring or trend formulas beyond the basic YoY / TTM / aggregate calculations, no automatic
  conclusions.
- Future: a separate China AI Capex sheet, kept out of this aggregate, for independent US/China
  comparison.
