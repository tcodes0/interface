Step 4 — TSMC / Advanced Manufacturing
This one can be fairly simple. I’d split it into two related indicators:
1. TSMC HPC revenue
2. CoWoS / advanced packaging utilization and capacity
1. TSMC HPC revenue — primary
TSMC reports revenue by platform, including HPC.
Track quarterly:
Quarter	HPC Revenue	HPC YoY Growth	HPC % of Revenue	Notes	Source	Last Updated
The most important field is HPC YoY growth.
We don’t need to attempt to isolate AI revenue from HPC. HPC includes other applications, but the category is sufficiently exposed to AI accelerators that it provides a useful upstream proxy.
Source:
* TSMC quarterly earnings releases
* TSMC investor presentations / financial reports
Signal:
HPC growth accelerating → bullish
HPC growth decelerating → warning
HPC revenue declining → bearish
I’d also keep total TSMC revenue growth available as context, but it doesn’t need to be a core field.
2. CoWoS — capacity / utilization
This is trickier because TSMC doesn’t give us a nice monthly “CoWoS utilization = 94%” number.
So, like HBM pricing, we shouldn’t manufacture precision that doesn’t exist.
Track qualitatively:
Quarter/Month	CoWoS Demand	Capacity / Expansion	Utilization	Source	Notes
	Tight / Normal / Weak
I’d focus on three things:
Demand / orders
* Strong customer demand
* Capacity fully booked
* Orders increasing/decreasing
Utilization
* High / tight
* Normalizing
* Falling
Capacity
* New CoWoS capacity coming online
* Expansion plans
* Geographic/fab expansion
The important distinction is:
Capacity expansion ≠ bearish.
We only care when:
capacity expansion + weakening orders/utilization
occur together.
Sources
HPC revenue
* TSMC earnings releases
* TSMC investor presentations
CoWoS
* TSMC earnings calls / investor commentary
* TSMC announcements
* Credible semiconductor research, particularly TrendForce
* Potentially Reuters/Bloomberg for customer/order developments when primary data isn’t available
I’d give TSMC’s own commentary priority and use industry sources to fill in the gaps.
Cadence
HPC: quarterly, whenever TSMC reports.
CoWoS: monthly/quarterly depending on data availability, but realistically quarterly is sufficient for our dashboard. We don’t need to chase every capacity announcement.
So the sheet can stay small:

CoWoS

The key bearish combination is:
TSMC HPC growth decelerating + CoWoS demand/utilization weakening.
And the strongest signal would be if that happens after Big Tech capex and GPU/HBM indicators have already started deteriorating. That would give us confirmation moving upstream through the physical supply chain.
